Grammar Reference Guide

Modals of Deduction (Present)

  • Must + base verb: High certainty (+). You are 90%+ sure something is true.

    "This server rack is glowing red; it MUST BE overheating."

  • Can't + base verb: Strong negative certainty (-). You are sure something is impossible.

    "That output CAN'T BE correct; the logic has a flaw."

  • Might / Could / May + base verb: Possibility (30-50% sure).

    "The network failure MIGHT BE caused by a DDoS attack."

Modals of Past Deduction

  • Must have + past participle: Certain past deduction.

    "The database crashed; hackers MUST HAVE exploited a vulnerability."

  • Can't have + past participle: Impossible past action.

    "He CAN'T HAVE sent that email; his account was offline."

  • Might have / Could have + p.p.: Possible past event.

    "The algorithm MIGHT HAVE misclassified the raw image."

Future Predictions & Likelihood

  • Be bound to + verb: Inevitable or almost certain based on present evidence.

    "AI integration IS BOUND TO change workplace productivity."

  • Be (un)likely to + verb: High probability or improbability.

    "Quantum computing IS LIKELY TO disrupt standard encryption."

  • Will be + -ing (Future Continuous): Action in progress at a specific future time.

    "In 2030, millions WILL BE WORKING alongside humanoid robots."
